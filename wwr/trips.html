Extraordinary Experience

If you are looking to kick back, catch some sun, and just enjoy being out on the water, this is the one for you. Over three relaxing hours, we’ll drift down a beautiful, calm stretch of the canyon where the currents are smooth and the splashes are light and playful. It is the perfect trip for families with little ones, grandparents, or anyone who just wants to unplug from the world for an afternoon. For $50, you get to sit back, watch for wildlife along the cliffs, and let the river do all the heavy lifting while you completely unwind

This is our absolute favorite for a full day of genuine river fun. The Grand River Expedition is a 6-hour journey that hits the absolute sweet spot of white-water, you’ll get plenty of exciting, bouncy wave trains that will get you completely soaked, but nothing too intimidating. You and your crew will be paddling together, laughing, and working as a team to guide the raft through twisting canyon channels. At $95 per person, it includes a great mix of high-energy splashes and calm stretches.

Let’s be honest: this trip is not for everyone. Devil’s Canyon is a fast, loud, and heart-pounding run built straight for adrenaline lovers who want a real physical challenge. You will be paired with our most experienced river captains to tackle massive, roaring waves, steep drops, and tight, rocky gardens where every single paddle stroke counts. You are going to work hard, you are going to get absolutely drenched, and you will need to give it everything you've got. If you love pushing your limits and want to experience more
