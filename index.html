Utah

Utah is a place that just makes you want to get outside. Up north, you have huge mountains with amazing snow for skiing. Down south, the dirt turns bright red, and it looks like you are walking on another planet. Everyone here loves to hike, camp, and explore. It is a very friendly and quiet place, but the views are so big and beautiful they will take your breath away
